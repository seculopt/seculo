Finding a property shouldn't feel like a second job

Finding the right property in Portugal means searching across ImóVirtual, Idealista, SuperCasa, and a dozen regional portals. Each with its own filters, its own format, and no way to see everything side by side. Século Explorador changes that.

The Method

How Século works

  1. 01

    Tell us what you're looking for

    Location, property type, price, size, bedrooms. Set your filters once and you're done. No repeating yourself across websites.

  2. 02

    We cover the whole Portuguese market, so you don't have to

    We search across every major portal at once. Results are scored by relevance, pinned on a map and enriched with nearby amenities, ready for you to explore.

  3. 03

    Explore on the map, export when ready

    Browse results on an interactive map with real planning layers: land classification, flood zones, wildfire history, drive-time isochrones and nearby amenities. When you've found your shortlist, download it as Excel or CSV in one click.
